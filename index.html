While Avery plays: options for the boys and grandparents, best to worst

  • 1. Meow Wolf Convergence Station, 20 minutes. Timed entry, book ahead. Saved for this crew, and built for exactly these ages.
  • 2. iFLY indoor skydiving, Lone Tree, 30 minutes. The boys fly, the grandparents watch from the couches. Book ahead.
  • 3. Boondocks Food and Fun, 25 minutes. Go-karts, mini golf and an arcade for a longer window.
  • 4. Wings Over the Rockies Air and Space Museum, 15 minutes. A hangar full of aircraft plus flight simulators.
  • 5. Denver Museum of Nature and Science, 20 minutes. Dinosaurs, space and an IMAX. The Denver Zoo sits in the same park.
  • 6. Water World, Federal Heights, 30 minutes. Top pick for the boys, the most work for the grandparents.
  • 7. Topgolf Centennial, 25 minutes. Covered bays, and Grandpa gets to swing too.
  • 8. Bass Pro Shops at Northfield, 10 minutes. Giant aquarium, arcade shooting gallery, free to wander. Movies and food are steps away.
  • 9. Rocky Mountain Arsenal National Wildlife Refuge, 15 minutes. Bison from the car, best in the morning. More the grandparents' speed than the boys'.
  • 10. Downtown Aquarium, 25 minutes. Easy two hours, air conditioned.
  • 11. Stanley Marketplace and the hotel pool for the short gaps between games.
  • Monday everyone is at the fields for the 11 a.m. final. No side trips on championship day.
Altitude notes: Denver sits at 5,280 feet and Georgetown at 8,500. Push water all week, especially for Avery. Sunscreen goes farther up here than it does in Virginia.
